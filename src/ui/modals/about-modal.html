Padify — Offline capable

Install

Padify is an offline-capable, no-frills notepad which is a Progressive Web App as well. It is privacy-focused — never collecting your precious data, light-weight — loading almost instantly, and it keeps working with zero internet.

New here? Take a quick tour of everything Padify can do — writing, focus tools, sounds, snapshots, sync and more.

CAUTION: Take a backup of your notes more often since it's all stored on your browser.
